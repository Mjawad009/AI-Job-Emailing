Sending method
This app requests two Gmail permissions: sending mail, and reading your inbox (used only to spot delivery-failure notifications for bounce tracking). Seeing "Google hasn't verified this app"? That's expected in Testing mode — click Advanced, then Go to [app name] (unsafe). Only accounts added as test users in Google Cloud Console can connect at all.
Works with any provider that offers SMTP credentials — Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Zoho, your host's mail server, etc. Password is stored but never shown back — leave blank when saving to keep the one already on file.
Bounce detection
Runs automatically every few minutes for connected accounts. This is best-effort — it scans your inbox for delivery-failure emails and matches them by recipient address, since Gmail has no dedicated bounce API. Some bounces may be missed or take a while to register.
